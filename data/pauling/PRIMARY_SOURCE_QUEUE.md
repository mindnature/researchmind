# Primary Source Queue

These are the highest-priority archival objects blocking promotion of Pauling Episodes to `high` confidence and heuristics to `validated`.

## P0 — Alpha helix contemporaneous process evidence

Goal: directly inspect the earliest surviving March 1948 alpha-helix drawing/notes and related 1948–1951 correspondence.

Need to establish:

- exact provenance/date of the surviving drawing or reconstruction;
- which constraints were written down contemporaneously;
- whether the 5.1 Å vs 5.4 Å conflict was explicitly treated as a reason to delay publication;
- division of work among Pauling, Corey and Branson.

Archive gateway:

- http://scarc.library.oregonstate.edu/coll/pauling/proteins/index.html

## P0 — DNA late-1952 notebooks and correspondence

Goal: inspect the actual November–December 1952 sequence of model changes rather than rely on later historical reconstruction.

Need to establish:

- when the phosphate-core topology became the preferred model;
- documented crowding/steric/charge concerns before submission;
- what Corey's calculations changed;
- what evidence or time pressure affected the decision to submit;
- what Pauling knew about water content, diameter and higher-quality diffraction evidence at each decision point.

Known archive object:

- Pauling to Watson and Crick, 27 March 1953 — `sci9.001.34`
- https://scarc.library.oregonstate.edu/coll/pauling/dna/corr/sci9.001.34-lp-watsoncrick-19530327.html

Archive gateway:

- http://scarc.library.oregonstate.edu/coll/pauling/dna/index.html

## P0 — Harvey Itano 1948 research report

Goal: directly verify the experimental-choice chronology behind the sickle-cell Episode.

Known locator:

- Harvey A. Itano, `Research Report for Admission to Candidacy`, April 1948, p.10.
- Ava Helen and Linus Pauling Papers, `I: Individual Correspondence, Harvey A. Itano`.

Need to establish:

- the exact wording and rationale for proposing electrophoresis;
- which alternative assays were under consideration;
- whether sample scarcity explicitly influenced method choice in the primary document;
- Pauling/Itano division of decision ownership.

## P1 — Pauling–Burch blood-sample correspondence

Known locator from archival scholarship:

- George Burch correspondence, 10–25 May 1949.

Need to establish:

- when reliable sickle-cell blood supply became available;
- how sample constraints affected the experimental sequence;
- whether this changed the pace or choice of electrophoretic tests.

## Promotion rule

Do not close a P0 item merely because a later historian quotes the object. Close it only after the primary object itself is inspected or a faithful institutional scan/transcript is inspected and registered with a stable locator.
